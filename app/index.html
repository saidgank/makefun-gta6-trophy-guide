    Roadmap to the platinum

    Five stages, built from the official descriptions: story first (catching the missable moments as they pass), then the character strands, the state-wide counts and collectibles, the money and mayhem, and finally the 100% sweep. No stage needs anything from a later one.

    Missable trophies — catch these as they pass

    Most of the list can be earned whenever you like. These are the exceptions: the official descriptions tie them to a single mission, scene or retry, so this guide flags them missable until chapter replay is proven to cover them. Do them the first time the moment comes.
